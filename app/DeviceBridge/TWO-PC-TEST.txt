DEVICEBRIDGE 0.7 — PHYSICAL ACCEPTANCE CHECK

Use version 0.7 on BOTH PCs. Exit old versions completely first. The desk PC is
PC A (keyboard, mouse, headphones and microphone); the Ethernet PC is PC B.

1. Pair with a fresh six-character key, or reconnect the saved pair. If needed,
   use the connection address under More options. Verify both show Connected.
2. Keep the session running for two minutes. Minimize, maximize and restore each
   window. Close one connected window with X: sharing should continue in the tray.
   Open DeviceBridge again to restore that session without another key.
3. Choose the other screen's position on PC A. Move across that edge and back.
   Try typing, dragging and scrolling. No Start button or F8 is required.
4. On PC A open Check my setup and ask PC B to send the headphone test. Confirm
   the tone is audible through PC A's selected headphones. This checks network
   and playback. Then play browser/music audio on PC B to verify actual capture.
5. Pause other audio on PC B. Speak into PC A's mic and confirm its outgoing
   level moves. If not, select the correct Microphone to share in DeviceBridge.
6. Confirm PC B's Shared microphone received level moves. In its calling app or
   microphone-test app choose CABLE Output and confirm the same voice arrives.
   Test microphone and system sound together too; report any echo or mixed audio.
7. Copy the diagnostic report on both PCs. Reports include device names, default
   devices, packet counts and errors, not keys, typed text or recorded audio.
8. Verify explicit Disconnect, tray Disconnect and quit, and Ctrl+Alt+Esc stop
   sharing. Reopen and verify saved reconnection. X alone should keep sharing.

Do not treat a moving meter or automated tests as proof of what a calling app
selected. Physical listening/voice confirmation is required. No audio is saved
by the built-in level checks. This preview remains unsigned; Windows may warn.
